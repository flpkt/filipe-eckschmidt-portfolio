How I think

Solve the right problem, then make it simple.

Five things I hold myself to on every project.

  1. 01

    Problem first, pixels second

    Most of my early time goes into framing the problem. When it's framed well, most design decisions become obvious.

  2. 02

    Facts over opinions

    Analytics, user interviews and support tickets settle debates. Judgment and taste decide the rest.

  3. 03

    Process is a toolset, not a script

    I pick the method the problem needs. Sometimes that's a round of interviews; sometimes it's a data pull and a prototype by Friday.

  4. 04

    Own it end to end

    A missing spec, an unhandled edge case, a broken component: I sort it out instead of leaving it for someone else.

  5. 05

    Decide, ship, measure

    Make the call with the best evidence available, track what happens, and adjust.

Toolset

Picked per problem, not run in order.

No fixed flow. These are the methods I reach for, and when.

Product analytics

When I need to know what people actually do. Funnels and usage data before opinions.

User interviews

When I need to know why. Numbers show where it breaks; people explain it.

Usability & A/B tests

When a decision is risky or expensive to undo, or two good options need a real answer.

Prototyping

When the interaction is the question. Figma prototypes, or quick HTML/CSS builds with AI tools.

Specs & edge cases

When engineering needs the rules, not just the screens. States, priorities, what happens when.

Design systems

When the same problem keeps coming back. Tokens and components, built to be reused.
